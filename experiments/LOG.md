## Cross-cutting finding: public LB rank is not safe to use for final submission choice
**#8 has a worse PUBLIC score than #6 (0.60328 vs 0.63809) but a better PRIVATE score (0.64542 vs
0.6252)** — picking a "final" submission by public rank alone would have picked the wrong one here.
If this competition's final standing is decided by private score (typical Kaggle format), **#8 is
currently the best-known submission in this project, not #6**, despite #6 looking better on every
leaderboard view available during the competition. Re-check this before any final submission
decision — don't default to "the public-best one."
